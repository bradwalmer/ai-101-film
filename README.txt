Alvernia University: The Commuter Parking Guide (3D version)

How to watch:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click alvernia-commuter-parking-guide-3d.html to open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Press Play and turn your sound on.

Notes:
- You need an internet connection. The page loads the Three.js 3D library (version r128) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
- The narrator uses your browser's built-in voice, so it sounds different on each computer.
- Runtime is about 2 minutes 13 seconds.
- Student parody. Not an official Alvernia University video.
